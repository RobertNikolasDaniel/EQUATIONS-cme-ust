present value

PV = CF / (1+r)^t

future cash flow discounted back to today

---

coupon payment

C = (c * F) / 2

semiannual treasury coupon

---

dirty price

P =
Σ [ C / (1 + y/2)^i ]
+
F / (1 + y/2)^N

full treasury value

---

clean price

Pclean = Pdirty - AI

quoted treasury price

---

accrued interest

AI = C * (d / D)

interest earned since last coupon

---

yield relationship

yield up -> price down

yield down -> price up

inverse relationship

---

yield to maturity

P =
Σ [ CFi / (1 + y/2)^i ]

solve for y

---

macaulay duration

DMac =
Σ [ ti * PVCFi ] / P

weighted timing of cash flows

---

modified duration

DMod =
DMac / (1 + y/2)

rate sensitivity

---

price change approximation

ΔP ≈ -DMod * P * Δy

duration approximation

---

dv01

1bp dollar sensitivity

---

convexity

ΔP/P
≈
-DMod * Δy
+
0.5 * Convexity * (Δy)^2

---

no coupon payments

---
