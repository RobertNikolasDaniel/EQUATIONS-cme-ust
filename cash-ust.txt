# 01 — Cash UST Pricing Equations

## Cash Flow

\[
C=\frac{c \times F}{2}
\]

\[
CF_i=C
\]

\[
CF_N=C+F
\]

---

## Discount Factor

\[
DF_i=\frac{1}{(1+\frac{y}{2})^i}
\]

---

## Dirty Price

\[
P_{dirty}=\sum_{i=1}^{N} CF_i \times DF_i
\]

\[
P_{dirty}
=
\sum_{i=1}^{N}
\frac{C}{(1+\frac{y}{2})^i}
+
\frac{F}{(1+\frac{y}{2})^N}
\]

---

## Closed Form

\[
P_{dirty}
=
C
\left(
\frac{1-(1+\frac{y}{2})^{-N}}
{\frac{y}{2}}
\right)
+
F(1+\frac{y}{2})^{-N}
\]

---

## Accrued Interest

\[
AI=C \times \frac{d}{D}
\]

---

## Clean Price

\[
P_{clean}=P_{dirty}-AI
\]

---

## Dirty Price From Clean Price

\[
P_{dirty}=P_{clean}+AI
\]

---

## Yield Equation

\[
P_{dirty}
=
\sum_{i=1}^{N}
\frac{CF_i}{(1+\frac{y}{2})^i}
\]

Solve for:

\[
y
\]

---

## Price Change

\[
\Delta P \approx -D_{mod}P\Delta y
\]

---

## DV01

\[
DV01 \approx D_{mod} \times P \times 0.0001
\]

---

## Modified Duration

\[
D_{mod}
=
\frac{D_{Mac}}
{1+\frac{y}{2}}
\]

---

## Macaulay Duration

\[
D_{Mac}
=
\frac{
\sum_{i=1}^{N}
t_i
\left(
\frac{CF_i}{(1+\frac{y}{2})^i}
\right)
}
{P_{dirty}}
\]

---

## Convexity Approximation

\[
\frac{\Delta P}{P}
\approx
-D_{mod}\Delta y
+
\frac{1}{2}Convexity(\Delta y)^2
\]

---

## One-Line Summary

\[
P_{clean}
=
\left[
\sum_{i=1}^{N}
\frac{CF_i}{(1+\frac{y}{2})^i}
\right]
-
AI
\]
