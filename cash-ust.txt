# cash ust equations

simple treasury caveman math

---

# present value

PV = CF / (1+r)^t

what this means:

future money worth less today

variables:

PV = present value

CF = future cash flow

r = discount rate / yield

t = time

---

# treasury coupon payment

C = (c * F) / 2

what this means:

treasuries pay coupons 2 times per year

variables:

C = coupon payment

c = annual coupon rate

F = face value

example:

4% treasury

C = (0.04 * 100) / 2

C = 2

bond pays:

2 dollars every 6 months

---

# dirty price

P =
Σ [ C / (1+y/2)^i ]
+
F / (1+y/2)^N

what this means:

full treasury value

all coupons discounted back to today

plus principal discounted back to today

variables:

P = dirty price

Σ = sum all coupon payments

C = coupon payment

y = yield

i = coupon payment number

F = face value

N = final payment period

---

# clean price

Pclean = Pdirty - AI

what this means:

market quoted treasury price

does NOT include accrued interest

variables:

Pclean = clean price

Pdirty = full bond value

AI = accrued interest

---

# accrued interest

AI = C * (d / D)

what this means:

interest earned since last coupon

variables:

AI = accrued interest

C = coupon payment

d = days since last coupon

D = total days in coupon period

---

# yield relationship

yield up -> price down

yield down -> price up

what this means:

higher yields discount cash flows harder

therefore bond prices fall

---

# yield to maturity

P =
Σ [ CFi / (1+y/2)^i ]

solve for y

what this means:

yield is the discount rate that makes all future cash flows equal current price

variables:

P = bond price

CFi = future cash flow

y = yield

i = payment period

---

# macaulay duration

DMac =
Σ [ ti * PVCFi ] / P

what this means:

weighted average timing of cash flows

roughly:

how long money trapped in bond

variables:

DMac = macaulay duration

ti = time of cash flow

PVCFi = present value of each cash flow

P = bond price

---

# modified duration

DMod =
DMac / (1+y/2)

what this means:

how sensitive bond price is to yield changes

this is trader duration

variables:

DMod = modified duration

DMac = macaulay duration

y = yield

---

# price change approximation

ΔP ≈ -DMod * P * Δy

what this means:

estimate price move from yield move

variables:

ΔP = change in price

DMod = modified duration

P = bond price

Δy = yield change

negative sign means:

yields up -> prices down

---

# dv01

DV01 ≈ DMod * P * 0.0001

what this means:

dollar value of 1bp yield move

variables:

DV01 = dollar value of 1bp

DMod = modified duration

P = bond price

0.0001 = 1 basis point

---

# convexity

ΔP/P
≈
-DMod * Δy
+
0.5 * Convexity * (Δy)^2

what this means:

duration alone not enough

bond price curve bends

convexity adjusts for curve shape

variables:

ΔP/P = percent price change

DMod = modified duration

Δy = yield move

Convexity = curvature adjustment

---

# zero coupon treasury

P = F / (1+y)^T

what this means:

no coupon payments

only final payment discounted back

variables:

P = price

F = face value

y = yield

T = time to maturity

---

# premium bond

P > F

what this means:

bond trades above par

usually because:

coupon > yield

---

# discount bond

P < F

what this means:

bond trades below par

usually because:

yield > coupon

---

# par bond

P ≈ F

what this means:

bond trades near face value

usually because:

yield ≈ coupon

---

# treasury caveman truth

bond price =
discounted coupons
+
discounted principal
